Not a forecast with error bars — 27 distinct worlds, visited one at a time.

Junior design employment vs the no-AI world — shown one simulated future at a time instead of as a band, because bands get read as "the forecast plus noise" when the truth is closer to "here are distinct worlds we can't yet choose between." Earlier futures linger as ghosts. One honesty note: these 27 are combinations from our assumption grid, not random draws from a forecast — they are equally spaced, not equally likely. The red-tinted runs are inelastic worlds, blue-tinted elastic; notice the color, not the wiggle, is what separates them.

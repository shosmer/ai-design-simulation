Method. 27 agent-based simulation runs (3 capability scenarios × demand elasticity 1.0–2.0 × 3 seeds), each differenced against a paired no-AI counterfactual with identical random events. Lines are medians; bands are min–max across all runs; series smoothed with a 5-month centered window for display.

Data. Capability curves anchored to O*NET design-occupation task statements joined to the Anthropic Economic Index; firm adoption calibrated to the Census Bureau's Business Trends and Outlook Survey; labor-market context from BLS OEWS and Indeed Hiring Lab. The AEI observes one AI assistant, so visual-production automation is undercounted. A model is an argument made precise — not a forecast.

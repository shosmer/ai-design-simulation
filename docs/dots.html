Designers as dots

The model isn't an equation — it's thousands of individual careers. Here are 400 of them, twice.

Fixed appetite (elasticity 1.0)
Growing appetite (elasticity 2.0)
● junior  ● mid  ● senior  (darker = more senior)  ○ looking for work  · left the profession  | dots appearing later = new graduates entering

Each dot is one simulated designer — the same ~400 people (sampled from several thousand agents, same random seed) living through two different worlds. Watch the same careers diverge: in both worlds people get laid off and rehired (churn is normal), but where the dots end up — and how many fade out of the profession entirely — depends on the appetite assumption, not on the AI.

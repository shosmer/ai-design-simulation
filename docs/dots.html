Designers as dots

The model isn't an equation — it's thousands of individual careers. Here are 400 of them, twice.

Each dot is one simulated designer, and dot positions correspond: the dot at row 3, column 7 on the left is the same individual as row 3, column 7 on the right — 400 people sampled with identical indices from the month-0 population, which is identical across both worlds (same random seed; the runs only diverge as AI economics kick in). New graduates who enter later differ by world and are not shown — their story is the pipeline charts. In both worlds people get benched and rehired (churn is normal); what differs is where the same careers end up, and that depends on the appetite assumption, not the AI.

Five kinds of design work

“AI capability” is five different curves, not one — and which curve your work sits on is most of your personal exposure.

— base scenario   ― light: slow scenario   –– dashed: fast scenario. Y axis: share of that work AI can perform in firms that have adopted it.

Find your own work here. The curves' relative heights come from data — official O*NET task statements for design occupations joined to measured AI usage (Anthropic Economic Index) — and the model never treats "AI capability" as one number. Design-to-code is far ahead; visual production is undercounted (the usage data observes a text-first assistant); strategy and judgment work barely registers and is capped lowest. The absolute level is the scenario assumption the other pages vary.

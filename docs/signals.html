The simulation says demand elasticity decides designers' future; these are the measured series that will reveal it. Lean chips use simple disclosed thresholds (ratio: ±2% over the trailing year; formation: +5% vs the 2022-24 plateau; postings: −3% y/y) — summaries, not statistical tests. No single card proves anything — the blue world announces itself as a pattern: output-per-hire rising, formation surging, postings stabilizing. The red world is the same cards with the signs flipped.
